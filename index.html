Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Summary

ViralKit AI has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking scripts. What you type is processed in your browser and is not sent to any server by this app.

What is stored on your device

  • Your theme choice and last-used options (language, platform, tone, audience).
  • Your recent history (up to 30 generations) and saved items.
  • A counter of how many generations you made.

This uses your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device through this app. You can remove it with Clear history, Remove all on the Saved page, or by clearing your browser's site data. If storage is blocked, the app still works but forgets everything when you close the tab.

Offline cache

The app may save its own files in your browser so it can open offline. These files contain no personal data.

Hosting

The company that hosts this website may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and request time. This is outside the control of the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
